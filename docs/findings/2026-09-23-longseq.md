# Sequence length does not fill the dimensions (rhythm-contrastive objective)

- **Date:** 2026-09-23
- **Branch:** `experiment/longseq`, cut from the dim-256 measurement commit so the readout is the same instrument. Worktree `C:\Users\spamw\rfe\RFE-Core2-longseq`. Code and logs committed after the fact (2026-09-27) from that worktree, where they had been left uncommitted.
- **Corpus:** a richer scratch corpus generated by `tools/longseq/gen_corpus.py` into `data/corpus/scratch/` (969 words, 4.14 content-pairs per line vs 1.69 in the live file). Live `rhythm_train.jsonl` / `rhythm_holdout.jsonl` hashes unchanged (`f5d60859…`, `61c93de1…`).
- **Status:** measured, **partial grid** (2 of 4 cells). Superseded as a lever by the completion objective.

## Question

The dim-256 run validated the lock but paid nothing: participation ratio stayed near 4 at both 128 and 256. Samuel's diagnosis: short 2–3 word bags never give the extra dimensions anything to hold. Does sequence length plus vocabulary fill them?

## Result

Same recipe, seed, and epoch budget as `2026-09-23-dim256-substrate`. The short-corpus numbers are that finding's paired runs.

| corpus | dim | participation ratio | eff. rank (512 cap) | worst-pair centroid cos |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| short (dim256 finding) | 128 | 3.90 | 1.68 | — |
| short (dim256 finding) | 256 | 3.57 | 1.72 | — |
| **long** | 128 | **3.375** | 1.668 | 0.497 |
| **long** | 256 | **3.457** | 1.794 | 0.555 |

Rhythm accuracy stays ~0.995 and within-rhythm centroid cosine 0.989–0.999 in every cell: the rhythms are learned, and each one collapses onto a point.

The in-run short cells were not completed (`train_short_d128` stopped at epoch 5/20; `train_short_d256` never started) because the arc moved to the completion objective the same night. The comparison above uses the dim256 finding's short runs, which share the recipe.

## Verdict

Length and vocabulary do not fill the dimensions under the rhythm-contrastive objective. Long is not above short at either width, and 256 is not above 128. The contrastive loss spends any available width pulling each rhythm onto a point, so the bottleneck is the **objective**, not the data length or the dimension. That pointed at the completion (CBOW) objective, which did fill them (participation 3.4 → 35 @128 / 63 @256; `SPEECH_CORTEX_PROGRAM.md`).
